Where we come from

MMF started as a hands-on shop: machining, fabrication and machine design-and-build. Behind that work is [X] years of CAD experience, from single parts to full machine assemblies, plus automation built for real equipment.

Why Axiom

Some parts are too complex, or change too often, to draw by hand. Computational design solves that by encoding the engineering rules and generating the geometry from them. Axiom is the division that does this work. Because it sits inside a shop, every design is checked against how it will actually be made.

Our flagship project, RDE-2, was designed end to end this way. We share images and outcomes only; technical details are withheld for export-control reasons.
