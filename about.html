About

Built in a machine shop.

Milford Machining & Fabrication is an Ontario business registered in 2023 in Prince Edward County. Axiom is its computational design division, serving the Bay of Quinte region and Eastern Ontario.

Where we come from: a PEC machine shop

Behind MMF is more than ten years of CAD and machining, alongside industrial machinery design, fabrication, installation, machine safety and automation controls. We take equipment from first sketch to a commissioned, wired and documented machine on the floor.

Why Axiom

Some parts are too complex, or change too often, to draw by hand. Computational design solves that by encoding the design rules and generating the geometry from them. Axiom is the division that does this work.

It is grounded in hardware we have designed, built and installed: positioning systems, material handling, control panels, solar trackers, off-grid power and structures. Every rule we write is checked against how the part will be cut, welded, wired and maintained.

Our flagship project, RDE-2, was designed end to end this way. We share images and outcomes only; technical details are withheld for export-control reasons. Read how it was designed →
