FAQ

Common questions

Do you work with people and businesses outside Prince Edward County?

Yes. Design work is done remotely for clients anywhere in Canada. On-site work and commissioning are centred on Prince Edward County, Belleville, Quinte West, Kingston, Napanee, Brighton and Cobourg, and the wider Eastern Ontario area. Travel time is billed as listed on the pricing page.

What file types can you deliver?

STEP, IGES, SolidWorks, Rhino .3dm, DWG/DXF and PDF for CAD work, and STL or 3MF for 3D printing. Computational design is delivered as native Rhino .3dm.

Can you design parts for 3D printing or additive manufacturing?

Yes. We model and prepare parts for polymer and metal 3D printing, including lattices and internal channels. We design and prepare the files; the print itself is normally done by a printer or service bureau you choose.

Do you program PLCs, ESP32 and Shelly devices?

Yes. We program AutomationDirect CLICK PLCs and ESP32 controllers and configure Shelly devices for monitoring and control on small machines, farms, wineries, shops and homes. Our scope is design, programming and low-voltage control work. Any mains or line-voltage wiring is done by a licensed electrician, yours or one we arrange.

Do you do robotics?

We design and build automation, motion control and mechanisms, such as gantries, actuators and slew-drive solar trackers, together with the controls that run them.

Can you make a part from a sketch or an old part?

Yes. We can model it from a sketch, a PDF or the physical part, then machine or fabricate it at MMF. This is as-built and reverse modelling of the part, not a certified or stamped review.
