02 — Capabilities

A

Computational design

Algorithmic and voxel geometry built on the open-source PicoGK kernel: lattices, conformal channels, part families generated from rules.

B

Native .3dm delivery

Results arrive as clean Rhino files your team can open, edit and fabricate from. No proprietary viewer, no lock-in.

C

Automation & controls

Right-sized controls for small manufacturers: CLICK PLCs, Shelly and ESP32 monitoring, panel builds, installation and machine safety, with documented I/O.

D

CAD & drafting

Production-ready models, assemblies and drawings, plus accurate as-built 3D capture of rooms and equipment.

03 — Flagship

RDE-2

A methalox rotating detonation engine, designed end-to-end in Axiom.

RDE-2 is our proving ground: a geometry too complex to draw by hand, generated entirely from engineering rules and exported as a manufacturable model. It shows what the method can carry.

  • Fully computational geometry
  • Single-source rule set
  • Native .3dm output
  • Designed for additive manufacturing

Technical details are withheld. Propulsion data may be subject to Canadian and US export controls.
