01 — Position

We don't redraw parts.

We write the rules that produce them.

Change a requirement, regenerate the design.

Every output traceable to its inputs.

Then we make it real, in our own shop.

02 — Capabilities

A

Computational design

Generative and algorithmic geometry built on the open-source PicoGK kernel: lattices, conformal channels and part families generated from rules, ready for 3D printing and additive manufacturing.

B

Native .3dm delivery

Results arrive as clean Rhino files your team can open, edit and fabricate from. No proprietary viewer, no lock-in.

C

Automation & controls

Right-sized automation for small shops, farms, wineries and homes: CLICK PLC and ESP32 programming, Shelly monitoring, motion control and robotics-style mechanisms, low-voltage control and panel work, with documented I/O. Mains wiring by a licensed electrician.

D

CAD design & 3D modelling

Production-ready CAD drafting, 3D modeling, assemblies and drawings, plus as-built capture of rooms, parts and equipment.

03 — Flagship

RDE-2

A methalox rotating detonation engine, designed end-to-end in Axiom.

RDE-2 is our proving ground: a geometry too complex to draw by hand, generated entirely from design rules and exported as a manufacturable model. It shows what the method can carry.

  • Fully computational geometry
  • Single-source rule set
  • Native .3dm output
  • Designed for additive manufacturing

Technical details are withheld. Propulsion data may be subject to Canadian and US export controls.
